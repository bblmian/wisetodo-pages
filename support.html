WiseToDo 支持

我们随时准备帮助您

如果您在使用 WiseToDo 时遇到任何问题，或有功能建议，请通过以下方式联系我们。我们会尽快回复您。

常见问题

  • 语音识别不准确？请确保在安静环境下使用，并检查麦克风权限是否已开启（设置 → 隐私与安全 → 麦克风）。
  • 任务未同步到日历？请检查日历权限是否已开启（设置 → 隐私与安全 → 日历），并确保选择了正确的日历账户。
  • 到点没有提醒我？WiseToDo 自己不发通知——填了时刻的事情会被写进系统「提醒事项」App，由 iOS 按时提醒你。请检查：① 设置 → 隐私与安全 → 提醒事项，确认已允许 WiseToDo 访问；② 打开系统「提醒事项」App 看那条在不在。没有填具体时刻的事情不会被推送（一条不会响的提醒没有意义）。
  • 应用崩溃或无响应？请尝试退出并重新启动应用。如问题持续，请通过邮件联系我们并描述复现步骤。

订阅管理

  • 如何取消订阅：订阅由 Apple 管理，不在 WiseToDo 内取消。打开 iPhone「设置」→ 点最上方你的姓名 → 「订阅」→ 选择 WiseToDo → 「取消订阅」。取消后仍可使用到当前订阅周期结束。
  • 如何申请退款：退款同样由 Apple 处理。访问 reportaproblem.apple.com，用你的 Apple ID 登录，找到该笔购买并提交退款申请。
  • 换了设备订阅没了？在 WiseToDo 的订阅页点「恢复购买」，用同一个 Apple ID 即可恢复。

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Voice recognition is inaccurate? Try using the app in a quiet environment and check that microphone permission is enabled (Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone).
  • Tasks not syncing to Calendar? Check that calendar permission is enabled (Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars) and that you've selected the correct calendar account.
  • Didn't get reminded on time? WiseToDo does not send notifications of its own — anything with a specific time is written into the system Reminders app, and iOS delivers the alert. Check: (1) Settings → Privacy & Security → Reminders, make sure WiseToDo is allowed; (2) open the system Reminders app and confirm the item is there. Items without a specific time are not pushed (a reminder that can never fire is worse than none).
  • App crashes or freezes? Try force-quitting and relaunching the app. If the issue persists, please email us with steps to reproduce.

Managing your subscription

  • How to cancel: Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not inside WiseToDo. Open iPhone Settings → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions → select WiseToDo → Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the end of the current billing period.
  • How to request a refund: Refunds are also handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find the purchase and submit a refund request.
  • Subscription missing on a new device? Tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen in WiseToDo, using the same Apple ID.
