WiseToDo 隐私政策

最后更新：2026年9月4日

三句话看完：

1. 你的任务、笔记、日记存在你自己的 iPhone 和 iCloud 里，开发者读不到。

2. 要用 AI 功能，你的语音和相关文字会发送给字节跳动（火山引擎）处理，处理服务器在中国大陆。

3. 第一次打开会明确问你同不同意；不同意也能用，而且随时可以在设置里改。

WiseToDo（以下简称「本应用」）由独立开发者运营。本应用没有账号系统，不要求注册或登录，也没有我们自建的服务器保存你的内容。

一、你的内容存在哪里

你创建的任务、笔记、日志、对话、项目等全部内容，存放在你的 iPhone 本地，并通过 Apple 的 iCloud（CloudKit 私有数据库）在你自己的设备之间同步。

这份数据在你自己的 iCloud 账户里，开发者无法读取。删除方法见第七节。

二、哪些内容会离开你的设备，发给谁

为了实现语音识别与 AI 功能，以下内容会发送至字节跳动（火山引擎）的服务。只有在你同意 AI 数据处理之后才会发送（见第六节）。

会离开设备的内容什么时候用来做什么
录音音频流你按住说话，或进入语音对话时实时语音识别、语音对话
语音转写后的文本同上理解你的意图，生成任务与日志
你输入或说出的提问你发送时回答问题、联网搜索问答
你为对话添加的照片你主动拍照或选图并发送时看懂图片内容并回答。发送时会一并附上你当前的任务清单与最近 12 轮对话，让助手知道你在说哪件事
你的笔记与日记正文你主动点「AI 改写」时；以及为支持语义检索而做向量化时改写文本；生成用于检索的向量
你在设置里填写的称呼（昵称）进行语音对话时让助手用这个名字称呼你

语义检索的向量索引只保存在你的设备上，从不离开设备；离开设备的是上表中被向量化的那段文本。

关于「助手记下来的事」

这不是我们另外收集的东西，而是从你自己说过的话里提炼出来的——比如你说过「别在晚上提醒我」，它会记下这条规则。这份记忆存在你的 iPhone 和你自己的 iCloud 里，开发者读不到；但它会随每一次 AI 请求一起发送，助手才知道该怎么对你。

关闭 AI 数据处理（见第六节）之后，它不再被发送。删除方法见第七节。

服务器在哪里（跨境传输）

上述处理由字节跳动运营的火山引擎服务完成，处理节点位于中国大陆。这意味着：如果你不在中国大陆，上述内容会离开你所在的国家或地区，并在中国大陆境内被处理。如果你不接受这一点，请在第六节所述的位置关闭 AI 数据处理——关闭后本应用不会向其发送任何内容。

对方会保留多久

  • 一次语音对话进行中：字节跳动的服务端会保留该次对话最近 20 轮问答，用于让助手记得上文。
  • 每次开始新的语音对话都会重新建立上下文，上一次对话的内容不会被带入下一次。
  • 文字问答、图片理解、改写、向量化均为即时处理并返回结果。
  • 我们自己不保存这些内容——我们没有用于保存它们的服务器。

我们对第三方的要求

字节跳动（火山引擎）作为我们的受托处理方，须对上述数据提供与本政策所述同等或更高的保护，且仅为「处理你的请求并返回结果」这一目的处理这些数据，不得用于本政策所述目的之外的用途。其隐私与合规说明见 火山引擎官网。

三、本应用不收集什么

  • 不收集位置信息、广告标识符（IDFA）、通讯录、浏览记录、健康数据。
  • 不要求也不收集邮箱、手机号——本应用没有账号系统。
  • 不含任何统计、埋点或广告组件，不进行跨 App 或跨网站追踪，因此本应用不会出现「允许 App 跟踪你的活动吗」这类弹窗。

说明：第二节列出的「昵称」「照片」等内容之所以在 App Store 的隐私标签中被标为「收集」，是因为 Apple 把「数据离开设备、且开发者或第三方可访问」定义为收集——即使我们自己并不保存它。本节与第二节合起来，就是这份标签的完整解释。

使用条款采用 Apple 标准最终用户许可协议：Terms of Use (EULA)。取消与退款的操作步骤见支持页面。

六、你的选择：同意、拒绝、随时撤回

第一次打开本应用时，会有一屏明确告诉你第二节的事，并让你选择同意或先不同意。

  • 不同意时仍然可以用：手打记事、日程、笔记、日记、时间统计、推送到系统「提醒事项」。
  • 不同意时用不了：说话记事、语音对话、AI 整理日志、联网问答。
  • 随时可以改：应用内「设置 → 关于 → 隐私与数据」里的「AI 数据处理」开关，两个方向都能切。关闭后，本应用不再向字节跳动发送任何内容。

七、保留与删除

删除你在本应用里的内容

  • 删单条：在应用内直接删除对应的任务、笔记、日记或对话。
  • 全部删除：删除本应用，然后在 iPhone 的「设置 → 点最上方你的姓名 → iCloud → 管理账户存储空间 → WiseToDo → 删除数据」中删除 iCloud 里的副本。两步都做完之后，这些内容不再保留，且无法恢复。
  • 助手记下来的事：随上面两步一并删除（它存在应用的数据里和你自己的 iCloud 里）。

已经发送给字节跳动的部分

见第二节「对方会保留多久」：语音对话的上下文只在该次对话内保留最近 20 轮，下一次对话不再使用；其余均为即时处理。关闭 AI 数据处理后不会再有新的发送。

WiseToDo Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

The short version:

1. Your tasks, notes and journals live in your own iPhone and iCloud. The developer cannot read them.

2. To use the AI features, your voice and related text are sent to ByteDance (Volcano Engine) for processing, on servers located in mainland China.

3. The app asks for your explicit permission on first launch. You can decline and still use the app, and you can change your mind at any time in Settings.

WiseToDo is operated by an independent developer. The app has no account system — no sign-up, no login — and no server of ours stores your content.

1. Where your content lives

Tasks, notes, journals, conversations and projects are stored locally on your iPhone and synced across your own devices through Apple iCloud (CloudKit private database).

That data lives in your own iCloud account; the developer cannot read it. See section 7 for how to delete it.

2. What leaves your device, and to whom

To provide speech recognition and AI features, the following is sent to ByteDance (Volcano Engine). Nothing is sent unless you have consented to AI data processing (see section 6).

What leaves your deviceWhenWhat it is used for
Recorded audio streamWhile you hold to speak, or during a voice conversationReal-time speech recognition and voice conversation
Transcribed textSame as aboveUnderstanding your intent, generating tasks and journal entries
Questions you type or speakWhen you send themAnswering, and web-search question answering

The vector index used for semantic search never leaves your device; what leaves is the text being embedded, as listed above.

About "what the assistant has noted about you"

This is not something we collect separately — it is distilled from things you yourself have said. If you tell it "don't remind me at night", it notes that rule. This memory lives on your iPhone and in your own iCloud, and the developer cannot read it; but it is sent along with every AI request, which is how the assistant knows how to treat you.

Once you turn off AI data processing (section 6), it is no longer sent. See section 7 for how to delete it.

Where the servers are (cross-border transfer)

This processing is performed by Volcano Engine, operated by ByteDance, on nodes located in mainland China. This means that if you are outside mainland China, the content listed above leaves your country or region and is processed in mainland China. If you are not comfortable with this, turn off AI data processing at the location described in section 6 — once off, the app sends nothing to them.

How long they keep it

  • During a single voice conversation: ByteDance's servers retain the most recent 20 question-and-answer turns of that conversation so the assistant can follow the thread.
  • Every new voice conversation starts a fresh context; content from a previous conversation is not carried into the next one.
  • Text answering, image understanding, rewriting and embedding are processed on the fly and the result is returned.
  • We do not retain any of it ourselves — we have no server on which to retain it.

What we require of the third party

ByteDance (Volcano Engine) acts as our processor and is required to provide the same or equal protection of your data as stated in this policy, and to process it solely for the purpose of handling your request and returning a result — not for any purpose beyond those described here. Their privacy and compliance statement is available at volcengine.com.

3. What the app does not collect

  • No location, no advertising identifier (IDFA), no contacts, no browsing history, no health data.
  • No email address or phone number is requested or collected — the app has no account system.
  • No analytics, telemetry or advertising components, and no tracking across apps or websites. You will therefore never see an "Allow the app to track your activity?" prompt.

Note: items such as your display name and attached photos appear as "collected" on the App Store privacy label because Apple defines collection as data leaving the device where the developer or a third party can access it — even when we retain none of it ourselves. Sections 2 and 3 together are the full explanation of that label.

5. Subscriptions

The app offers one paid tier, WiseToDo Plus (1,500 AI conversation credits a month), with three ways to pay: monthly and quarterly are auto-renewing subscriptions; Lifetime is a one-time purchase and is not a subscription — it does not renew. The free tier also comes with an allowance each period. Purchase, renewal and refunds are handled entirely by Apple; the developer never sees your payment information and only learns whether you are on Free or Plus.

Terms of Use are Apple's standard End User License Agreement: Terms of Use (EULA). Steps for cancelling and requesting a refund are on the support page.

6. Your choice: consent, decline, withdraw at any time

On first launch the app shows a screen stating plainly what section 2 describes, and asks you to agree or decline for now.

  • If you decline, the app still works: typing tasks, scheduling, notes, journals, time statistics, and pushing items to the system Reminders app.
  • What is unavailable if you decline: speaking to capture, voice conversation, AI-organised journals, and web-search answering.
  • You can change your mind at any time: the AI Data Processing switch under Settings → About → Privacy & Data works in both directions. Once off, the app sends nothing to ByteDance.

7. Retention and deletion

Deleting your content in the app

  • A single item: delete the task, note, journal entry or conversation in the app.
  • Everything: delete the app, then remove its iCloud copy at Settings → your name at the top → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → WiseToDo → Delete Data. After both steps nothing is retained, and it cannot be recovered.
  • What the assistant has noted about you: removed by those same two steps (it lives in the app's data and in your own iCloud).

What was already sent to ByteDance

See "How long they keep it" in section 2: a voice conversation's context is retained only within that conversation (most recent 20 turns) and is not used by the next one; everything else is processed on the fly. Once you turn off AI data processing, nothing further is sent.
