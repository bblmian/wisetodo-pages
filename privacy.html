WiseToDo 隐私政策

最后更新：2026年3月16日

感谢您使用 WiseToDo。本隐私政策说明我们如何收集、使用和保护您在使用本应用时的信息。请在使用本应用前仔细阅读本政策。

1. 我们收集的信息

WiseToDo 在您使用语音输入功能时会录制您的语音，并将语音数据发送至 OpenAI Whisper API 进行语音转文字处理。除此之外，您创建的待办事项、任务数据以及偏好设置均存储在您的本地设备上，不会上传至我们的服务器。

  • 语音数据：仅在您主动触发录音时收集，处理完毕后不予保留。
  • 任务数据：完全存储于本地设备，我们无法访问。
  • 日历数据：仅在您授予日历权限后访问，用于创建和同步日程事件，不会传输至第三方。

2. 信息的使用方式

我们收集的信息仅用于以下目的：

  • 将语音转换为文字，以便创建和管理您的待办事项。
  • 通过 AI 对任务进行智能解析、分类和时间估算。
  • （如您授权）将任务同步至您的系统日历。

3. 第三方服务

WiseToDo 使用以下第三方服务：

  • OpenAI API：用于语音转文字（Whisper）和任务智能解析（GPT）。您的语音和任务文本会按照 OpenAI 隐私政策 进行处理。

我们不会向任何其他第三方出售、共享或披露您的个人信息。

4. 数据安全

您的任务数据存储在本地设备，受设备系统安全机制保护。语音数据通过加密连接传输至 OpenAI，处理后即丢弃，不在我们的服务器上保留。

5. 儿童隐私

WiseToDo 不面向 13 岁以下的儿童，我们不会有意收集未成年人的个人信息。

6. 权限说明

本应用可能请求以下系统权限：

  • 麦克风：用于语音输入功能，仅在您主动录音时激活。
  • 日历：用于将任务写入系统日历，仅在您授权后启用。

7. 政策变更

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。重大变更将通过应用内通知告知您。继续使用本应用即表示您同意更新后的政策。

WiseToDo Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 16, 2026

Thank you for using WiseToDo. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app. Please read this policy carefully before using the app.

1. Information We Collect

WiseToDo records your voice when you use the voice input feature and sends the audio to the OpenAI Whisper API for speech-to-text processing. Beyond that, your to-do items, task data, and preferences are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.

  • Voice data: Collected only when you actively trigger recording; not retained after processing.
  • Task data: Stored entirely on your local device; we have no access to it.
  • Calendar data: Accessed only if you grant calendar permission, used to create and sync events; never transmitted to third parties.

2. How We Use Information

Information we collect is used solely to:

  • Convert voice to text so you can create and manage your to-dos.
  • Intelligently parse, categorize, and estimate time for tasks using AI.
  • Sync tasks to your system calendar (only if you have granted permission).

3. Third-Party Services

WiseToDo uses the following third-party service:

  • OpenAI API: Used for speech-to-text (Whisper) and intelligent task parsing (GPT). Your voice and task text are processed in accordance with OpenAI's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell, share, or disclose your personal information to any other third parties.

4. Data Security

Your task data is stored on your local device, protected by the device's built-in security mechanisms. Voice data is transmitted to OpenAI over an encrypted connection and is discarded after processing — it is not retained on our servers.

5. Children's Privacy

WiseToDo is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

6. Permissions

The app may request the following system permissions:

  • Microphone: Required for voice input; activated only when you initiate a recording.
  • Calendar: Used to write tasks to your system calendar; enabled only upon your authorization.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated via an in-app notice. Continued use of the app constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
