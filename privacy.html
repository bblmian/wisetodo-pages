WiseToDo 隐私政策

最后更新：2026年8月7日

WiseToDo（以下简称「本应用」）由独立开发者运营。本应用没有账号系统，不要求注册或登录，也没有自建服务器保存你的内容。

一、你的内容存在哪里

你创建的任务、笔记、日志、对话、项目等全部内容，存放在你的 iPhone 本地，并通过 Apple 的 iCloud（CloudKit 私有数据库）在你自己的设备之间同步。

这份数据在你自己的 iCloud 账户里，开发者无法读取。卸载本应用并删除 iCloud 中的数据后，这些内容将不再保留。

二、哪些内容会被发送出去，发给谁

为了实现语音识别与 AI 功能，以下内容会被发送至字节跳动（火山引擎）的服务：

发送内容用途
录音音频流实时语音识别、语音对话
语音转写后的文本理解你的意图、生成任务与日志
你的提问联网搜索问答
笔记与日志正文AI 改写（你主动点击时）、向量化以支持语义检索

这些数据用于即时处理并返回结果。字节跳动对这些数据的处理适用其自身的隐私政策，建议你一并查阅。

语义检索的向量索引只保存在你的设备上，从不离开设备；离开设备的是上表中被向量化的那段文本。

三、我们不收集什么

本应用不收集：个人身份信息、位置、广告标识符（IDFA）、通讯录、浏览记录。

本应用不含任何统计、埋点或广告组件，不进行跨 App 追踪。

四、权限

权限用途
麦克风语音输入与实时语音对话
语音识别把你说的话实时转成文字
日历把你的日程写进系统「日历」
提醒事项把你要做的事推送到系统「提醒事项」，由系统按时提醒你
相机拍照作为对话附件
相册读：选择照片作为对话附件；写：把导出的日记图片保存到你的相册

每一项都只在你主动使用对应功能时触发，可随时在系统「设置」中撤销。

关于提醒：本应用自己不发送通知。需要按时提醒的事项，由本应用写入系统「提醒事项」，再由 iOS 在你设定的时间提醒你。因此本应用不会请求通知权限。

五、订阅

本应用提供自动续订订阅。购买、续订与退款全部由 Apple 处理，开发者不接触你的支付信息，只能得知你当前处于哪一档订阅。

六、儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集其信息。

七、变更与联系

本政策如有变更将更新本页面与「最后更新」日期。

WiseToDo Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

WiseToDo is operated by an independent developer. The app has no account system — no sign-up, no login — and no server of ours stores your content.

1. Where your content lives

Tasks, notes, journals, conversations and projects are stored locally on your iPhone and synced across your own devices through Apple iCloud (CloudKit private database).

That data lives in your own iCloud account; the developer cannot read it. After you delete the app and remove its data from iCloud, none of it is retained.

2. What leaves your device, and to whom

To provide speech recognition and AI features, the following is sent to ByteDance (Volcano Engine) services:

SentPurpose
Recorded audio streamReal-time speech recognition and voice conversation
Transcribed textUnderstanding intent, generating tasks and journals
Your questionsWeb-search question answering
Note and journal textAI rewriting (only when you tap it), and embedding for semantic search

This data is used to process your request and return a result. ByteDance's own privacy policy governs its handling; please review it as well.

The vector index used for semantic search never leaves your device; what leaves is the text being embedded, as listed above.

3. What we do not collect

No personal identifiers, no location, no advertising identifier (IDFA), no contacts, no browsing history.

The app contains no analytics, telemetry or advertising components and does not track you across apps or websites.

4. Permissions

PermissionPurpose
MicrophoneVoice input and real-time voice conversation
Speech RecognitionTranscribing what you say into text
CalendarWriting your events into the system Calendar app
RemindersPushing your to-dos into the system Reminders app, so iOS can alert you on time
CameraTaking a photo to attach to a conversation
PhotosRead: choosing a photo to attach. Write: saving an exported journal image to your library

Each is triggered only when you use the corresponding feature and can be revoked at any time in Settings.

About reminders: this app does not send notifications of its own. Anything that needs to alert you on time is written into the system Reminders app, and iOS delivers the alert. The app therefore does not request notification permission.

5. Subscriptions

The app offers auto-renewable subscriptions. Purchase, renewal and refunds are handled entirely by Apple; the developer never sees your payment information and only learns which tier you are currently on.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes and contact

Changes will be reflected on this page and in the "Last updated" date.
